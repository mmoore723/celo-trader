# Celo Trader Strategy Research Ledger

Last updated: 2026-09-01

This is the durable decision log for strategy research. A strategy is promoted only when its implementation is registered in the reviewed production catalog and its evidence artifact is present under `config/strategy_evidence/`. Rejected ideas remain listed so they can be revisited with a materially different hypothesis, dataset, or market regime instead of being unknowingly repeated.

## Required strategy-by-ticker validation matrix

No strategy result is assumed to transfer to another ticker. `PASS`, `FAIL`, and
`INCONCLUSIVE` below require an actual replay using executable historical option
quotes for that ticker. `DATA NEEDED` means that no adequate matching option-quote
history is currently stored; underlying candles alone cannot establish executable
option profitability.

The reviewed research universe is **SPY, QQQ, AMZN, AAPL, NVDA, and TSLA**. Dynamic
scanner discoveries remain research-only until the relevant strategy/ticker pair
has its own recorded evidence. A strategy may only run on a ticker explicitly
listed as approved in its evidence artifact and production catalog entry.
